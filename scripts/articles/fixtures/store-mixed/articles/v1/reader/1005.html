A sprocket bundle is a widget equipped with a chosen sprocket over each handle.

Every finite sprocket bundle has a trivialising cover, and any two trivialising covers of it agree.

The reader may picture this as a gadget glued along each of its handles.

The bundle map of a trivialising cover is natural in the chosen handle it refines.

The bundle map of a trivialising cover is natural in the chosen frame it refines.
